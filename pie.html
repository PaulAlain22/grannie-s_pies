Grandma's Lemon Meringue Pie

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Directions

Preheat Oven: Preheat oven to 350 degrees F.

Prepare Filling: Mix sugar, flour, cornstarch, and salt.

Bake Pie: Bake until golden brown.

Finish: Let pie cool before serving.
